# By-role attribution

Goalkeepers and outfield players observe different opportunities. Treating every touch as an identical event count rewards exposure and feed richness, not contribution. The [reducer](../../matchpulse/ratings.py) maintains explicit roles, active intervals and role history. A save uses a quality-weighted residual, a shot separates quality from outcome surprise, and a pass uses only its own before/after state. A later goal never supplies the pass's live feature. Receiver movement remains unsupported.

The save and shot residual coefficients are declared baseline choices. They are not fitted expert preferences. The fixture demonstrates a 0.42-quality save with contribution +0.21 and a role change from AM to CM. Those arithmetic checks validate implementation, not the fairness of the coefficients. Same-name identity and role revisions are independent issues: correcting a role must not remap a person.

The original recurrent diagnostic accepts role indicators, location support masks and causal sequence history. Analytic BPTT is checked by finite differences in [research tests](../../tests/test_research.py). A separate ridge adjusted plus-minus utility regularizes collinear lineup designs; the exact two-identical-column case has a symmetric independent solution. Neither component is promoted into served ratings. Actual by-role opportunity references, keeper distribution, defensive positioning and out-of-position calibration still need lawful event/tracking evidence. Event-only and full-context ablations remain blocked rather than treating unseen actions as zero.
