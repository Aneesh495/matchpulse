# Real-time correction and ticker integrity

A goal may be visible before a VAR decision. Erasing the first state destroys what the viewer actually saw; leaving its contribution in the rating double-counts an overturned event. The [journal](../../matchpulse/storage.py) keeps both envelopes, and the [reducer](../../matchpulse/ratings.py) excludes retracted targets only in the new corrected view. Earlier saved snapshots remain immutable. Revision IDs, reason codes and receipt times are shown in the terminal.

The authored walkthrough contains the provisional goal at v28, its retraction at v30, a late pass, a role correction and a shot-quality revision. The final score is 1 to 0 from a separate valid goal. The keeper contribution and red-card exposure are independently checked. These records test chronology mechanics under known truth, not actual provider behavior. A source-specific freshness SLO remains unavailable without a licensed live feed.

Completion is also revisable. A corrected end fact can make a previously completed match interrupted. The engine then publishes unavailable estimate versions and a ticker-retraction record. It does not leave valid-looking conditional medians in the current ticker. Conversely a metadata-only correction after completion recomputes against the frozen match prior and preserves exact zero changes rather than compounding the match twice. [Completion tests](../../tests/test_demo.py) exercise both paths. Publication shares the same transaction as journal persistence, so failure cannot leave an estimate whose triggering facts were rolled back.
