# ADR 005: Separate contribution display from player skill

Status: accepted for the local diagnostic profile.

A single rating easily acquires unwarranted authority. Event-only feeds omit tactical positioning and intent.

Decision: Use a declared contribution baseline, exposure shrinkage and visible support bands. Unsupported fields remain unsupported. Unused substitutes receive no rating. All untrusted or short appearances stay provisional.

Consequence: The local surface and bands are uncalibrated. Role-normalized learned calibration and blinded independent review are blocked, so numerical display precision must not be interpreted as empirical measurement precision.
