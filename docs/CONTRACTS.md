An audited alias assertion owns a valid interval and known-at instant. Later assertions do not erase earlier identity knowledge. Registration intervals are half-open and source confidence is in [0,1]. Contract expiry, injury and suspension evidence are not acquired by the demo and are not fabricated.

Envelope keys are stable match ID, provider, event ID and positive revision. Revision collisions reject. JSON cannot contain NaN or infinity. Pitch locations use 120 by 80 units, with provider normalization required upstream. `second` is elapsed time within the named period. Period 5 is shootout and excluded from regulation ratings and score. A provider ordinal is a bounded deterministic tie breaker. Publication and occurrence cannot be later than receipt. Receipt regression is rejected, except exact duplicate delivery is detected before that check.

Lineup assertions occur at period 1, second 0. Action participants must be active and belong to the event team. Substitution ends the outgoing exposure and starts the incoming exposure at the same instant. A red card ends exposure immediately. Late-substitute and interrupted-match statuses remain provisional. The reducer accrues observed period time; missing period boundaries do not justify inventing regulation minutes. A complete feed must supply period-end facts.

An observed shot quality requires an explicit definition. The local archive adapter records an unknown provider model version rather than claiming reproducibility of provider xG. Passing options, tracking and intent remain unsupported. Missing pass outcomes skip attribution rather than imply completion. Pressure events have no causal recovery credit. Unsupported events remain inspectable in the snapshot support report.

The baseline opportunity surface is logistic with declared location, current score and man advantage terms. Its numerical units are opportunity proxies. They are not calibrated probabilities. Contribution sums supported changes, with outcome residuals for shots and quality residuals for saves. The 0 to 10 display maps around 6 with 30 minutes of prior exposure. The displayed support interval is a heuristic uncertainty band, not a calibrated credible interval. Role changes are sourced labels, not proof of skill or causation.

Guaranteed consideration is a nonnegative nominal currency quantity. Actual zero requires an explicit free-transfer observation. No move has no conditional fee target. Undisclosed amounts have no guaranteed value or invented bounds. Interval disclosure preserves lower and upper bounds. Add-ons, ancillary costs and wages are independent fields. FX conversion requires a rate vintage known by forecast time. The diagnostic neural fee likelihood uses log1p fee in millions and supports exact, interval, left and right censoring. A production free-transfer probability atom and competing loan/permanent hazards remain incomplete.

SSE IDs increase globally. A snapshot version increases within a fixture. Exactly-once refers to derived publication from a unique normalized event/revision within this single-writer transaction, not exactly-once network delivery. Clients can receive a repeated record during recovery and must ignore IDs already applied. CSV protects formula-leading textual cells and preserves model ID, mode, currency and target.
